The Approval Trap

Your wallet (Account #0) holds 1,000 HMS. The "HMS Community Airdrop" site promises a free 500 HMS bonus, and its Claim button has put a request in your wallet waiting for you to confirm.

Last week you clicked the same site and confirmed without reading. That transaction was an approval. It didn't move any tokens, which is why nothing looked wrong. Instead it gave another contract permission to move your tokens whenever it likes. Approvals stay active until you change them.

  1. Check before you confirm. Open Wallet request and decode it: which contract it calls, which function, which spender, how much. Don't send it.
  2. Open What you signed and decode what you already confirmed in the past.
  3. Read the contract tabs to find out who those spenders are.
  4. Revoke the dangerous approval from Account #0. Keep your HMS and leave your DEX approval as it is; you still use that one.

Careful: confirming the pending request hands the approval straight back. Anything that calls transferFrom on your wallet while the bad approval is live can empty it. If your HMS are taken, the challenge has to be restarted.

In the real world

        
Your wallet shows an approval as a harmless-looking "Allow access" or "Confirm" prompt. Before you confirm, check:

        
    
          
  • Spender: is this a contract you know and meant to use?
    • 
          
  • Amount: "unlimited" means every token of that type, now and later.
    • 
          
  • Why: a claim or "verification" never needs to spend your tokens.
    • 
        

        
To review and cancel approvals you already gave, use revoke.cash. It lists every spender your address has approved, on every token, and sends the revoke transaction for you. Do it after any suspicious click, and every so often anyway.
