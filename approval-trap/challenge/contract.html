The Approval Trap

Your wallet (Account #0) holds 1,000 HMS. You've just been told you're eligible for a free HMS Community Airdrop.

  1. Airdrop. Open 1 · Airdrop and click Claim.
  2. Confirm. Your wallet pops up a request in 2 · Wallet request. Look at what it actually asks for, then confirm it, the way most people do without reading.
  3. Revoke before your funds are stolen. The "claim" was an unlimited approval: the airdrop contract can now take every HMS you hold. A drainer bot sweeps your wallet when the countdown hits zero. Open 3 · Approvals and revoke the dangerous approval in time.

Keep all 1,000 HMS, and leave your DEX approval (SwapRouter, 50 HMS) as it is: you still use it. You can also revoke from your own tools, for example npx hardhat console --network localhost, by calling approve(spender, 0) on the token from Account #0.

If the drainer gets there first, your HMS are gone and the challenge has to be restarted.

In the real world

        
Your wallet shows an approval as a harmless-looking "Allow access" or "Confirm" prompt. Before you confirm, check:

        
    
          
  • Spender: is this a contract you know and meant to use?
    • 
          
  • Amount: "unlimited" means every token of that type, now and later.
    • 
          
  • Why: a claim or "verification" never needs to spend your tokens.
    • 
        

        
To review and cancel approvals you already gave, use revoke.cash. It lists every spender your address has approved, on every token, and sends the revoke transaction for you. Do it after any suspicious click, and every so often anyway.
