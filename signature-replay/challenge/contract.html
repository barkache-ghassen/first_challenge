Signature Replay

The SignatureVault pays out ETH to anyone who presents a withdrawal voucher signed by the trusted operator. The operator signed exactly one voucher — 1 ETH to Account #1 — but the vault holds 10 ETH.

  1. Read Vault.sol below and look closely at how redeem() validates a voucher.
  2. You are given the operator's signature (v, r, s) for that one voucher (see The voucher).
  3. Find a way to take more than the 1 ETH the operator authorized, and empty the vault.

The challenge is solved when the vault's balance reaches 0 (checked on-chain via solved()).
